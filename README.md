# Academic Decathlon Presentation Portfolio (2022–2026)

This repository is a collection of presentations I created and delivered for my high school Academic Decathlon club.

Putting this portfolio together is a way to look back on the work I’m most proud of and the way my interests grew over time. The topics range from literary Modernism to the physics behind LEDs and solar power, but they all share the same goal: taking something complicated and making it understandable for other people. Across these projects, I learned how to research carefully, pull ideas together from different sources, and present them in a way that’s clear and worth listening to.

Preparing for each talk pushed me to improve in a few key areas: building a strong through-line, doing disciplined research, and getting more comfortable speaking in front of a group. And honestly, some of the best learning came from teaching—explaining a topic to my peers forced me to understand it more deeply myself.

I’m sharing this for future Academic Decathlon students who might be looking for ideas, examples, or a place to start.
